# Car Deals

Car Deals is a place to find used-car bargains near Madison, Wisconsin, and to sell a car of your own.

[Open Car Deals](https://krombyte.github.io/car-deals/)

Each listing shows a deal score and a market-value estimate. Bait prices and placeholders are filtered out, so the price you compare is a real asking price. Set your own minimum and maximum, or tap a quick range such as Under $5k.

Tap a photo to open a larger picture, the seller’s description, and how to reach them. Sign in to save cars and come back to them later. Sell your car with the form: details, photos, and the contact method you want buyers to use.

The help button is a Gemini-powered chatbot for filters, saved cars, listing a car, and your account.
