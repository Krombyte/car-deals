# Car Deals help

Car Deals lists used cars near Madison, Wisconsin. Asking prices are the seller's price. The estimate on a card is from similar listings in this dataset, adjusted for mileage. It is not a Kelley Blue Book value.

## Filters

- Type a minimum in Min and a maximum in Max. Leave either box empty for no limit.
- Under $5k, Under $10k, Under $20k, and Under $60k set the maximum and clear the minimum.
- Under 120k mi is a mileage chip, not a hard limit on what was collected.
- Make, Mileage, Distance, and Sort are the other dropdowns.
- Search matches make, model, trim, or city.

## Opening a listing

Click the photo or the card. The details stay on this page. Close, Esc, or the dark backdrop closes them. View listing opens the original post in a new tab. Craigslist does not publish the seller's name. Contact is through that listing. A private seller shows the name and the contact method they chose.

## Saving a car

The heart saves a car. Guests keep that list on this device. A signed-in account syncs it.

## Accounts

Sign in, Create account, and Reset password are on the Sign in button. Google sign-in is on that same screen. Verify your email before you list a car. Sign out is on the account screen after you sign in. If a method says it is not turned on, the owner still needs to enable it in Firebase Authentication.

## Selling

Sell opens the form. You need to be signed in and email-verified. Enter year, make, model, trim, price, mileage, title status, city, state, ZIP, description, up to 6 photos, and how buyers should contact you. Photos are resized in the browser to WebP and saved with the listing. Cloud Storage is not used. Only a clean title is published. Your car appears in Browse all with a Private seller badge. You can edit or delete it. Report listing sends a note about a listing.

## Mustang Picks

Mustang Picks shows Ford Mustangs from the same list. The photos are not personalized per viewer.

## Analytics

A notice lets you opt out. Opting out stops Google Analytics on this device. The choice is stored in this browser.
