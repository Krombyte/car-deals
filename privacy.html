Privacy

Back to Car Deals

Car Deals stores your theme and saved cars in this browser. If you sign in, your account and saved cars are stored in Firebase for project car-deals-d0ebf. Private listings and their photos are stored in Firestore. Photos are not uploaded to Cloud Storage.

With analytics left on, page views and clicks go to Google Analytics for Firebase (measurement ID G-8LJXWFY0MM). Advertising features are off. Opting out stops that collection on this device. The help chat, when it is available, sends your question and the FAQ to Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. It does not send the listing file or your email. No other third-party tracker is loaded.
